# SARCATSTIC PURRS — Locked Brand Contract

## Post formula
**One short relatable quote + Milo + exact footer**

## Milo
Primary source of truth: `assets/milo_collage.jpg`  
Supplementary reference: `assets/milo_profile.jpg`

Milo is the same recurring gray-and-white cat: plush gray fur, white muzzle/chest/paws, amber eyes with heavy sarcastic side-eye lids, pink nose/inner ears, small gold hoop on his right ear, yellow collar with round paw-print tag, compact chubby proportions.

## Quote typography
- Font: **Patrick Hand Regular**
- Color: **black**
- Weight: **regular only — never bold**

## Footer
Exact signature: **SARCATSTIC 🐾**
- black
- bottom center
- no tagline
- no extra line
- no extra footer copy

## Background families — exact locked palette
- **A — Warm Cream: #F7F1E8**
  - default / general Milo sarcasm
  - use most often
- **B — Dusty Sage: #C9D2C0**
  - dry humor, lazy moods, deadpan jokes
- **C — Muted Blue: #BDD6E7**
  - work, people, social-life sarcasm
- **D — Soft Blush / Peach: #F3CEC5**
  - relationships, dating, softer personal jokes

### Background treatment rules
- flat color or extremely subtle treatment only
- no strong gradients
- no busy background treatment
- Milo and black Patrick Hand Regular quote text must remain dominant
- Warm Cream is the default so the other three colors feel intentional when used

## Composition
- 4:5
- quote upper-left or upper-center
- Milo lower half, slightly off-center
- maximum 1–2 supporting objects
- quiet, minimal, non-distracting background
- no AI-generated text inside the illustration layer
